LinkLens

See where a link really goes before you click.

A Chrome extension that inspects links when you hover them: look-alike domains, letters from other alphabets, hidden destinations behind shortened links, and link text that lies. Everything runs locally; no link ever leaves your browser.

Who owns the site?

The card shows the full address with the part that decides ownership in bold. In paypal.com.account-review.xyz the owner is account-review.xyz.

Letters that lie

Punycode names are decoded, so xn--pypal-4ve.com shows up as pаypal.com with a Cyrillic "а", and is matched against frequently impersonated brands.

Install (2 minutes)

  1. Download linklens.zip from Releases and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
LinkLens is open source (MIT). Sample email and domains are made up for the demo.
